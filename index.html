Rules, subject coverage and sources

For Stream 1 only. Class 10: external theory after TMA weighting plus practical must reach 33% of the combined external maximum. Class 12: theory and practical must each reach 33% of their respective maximum. Both classes require at least 33/100 overall. TMA has no separate passing minimum. Enter total practical marks including any formative and summative components, not just the final practical test.

Raw theory is multiplied by 0.8; TMA weight is 20% of the theory maximum. Portal TMA is assumed to be out of 20; choose weighted mode if you already have marksheet TMA marks. Calculations use unrounded values. Near-threshold cases are flagged because published historical integer thresholds and rounding are not fully consistent with exact percentages. A conservative raw target is rounded upward; a pass estimate is not an official result. Do not count unattempted/absent external components as a genuine zero mark.

40 Class 10 and 47 Class 12 presets follow the academic prospectus Table 9. Six additional Class 12 entries are retained from the earlier calculator, but their allocation is not verified; enter the official theory maximum manually. You can override an allocation for an older syllabus. Custom calculations assume a 100-mark academic subject and the standard TMA scheme; separate vocational courses, TOC and special certification arrangements are not modelled.

Certification indicator checks whether at least five entered subjects meet the marks criteria with one or two languages in a qualifying group of five. It does not verify enrolment eligibility, subject clashes, TOC or other administrative requirements.
